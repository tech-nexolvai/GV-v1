# Widgets (draft)

**Draft starter, not yet checked with GV.** How to review shop drawings or submittals for widgets.
Read with `SKILL.md` (how to work) and `rules.md` (GV's numbers in force: they win over anything
here).

## Compare
Use this product's checklist: dimensions, specification values, finish and material codes,
quantities and every note. Compare each against the architect's drawing or specification. Where GV
has a standard that is not on the drawing, it is in `rules.md`; until one is published, report what
the vendor shows and say no GV standard is published yet.

## What counts as ours
The widget itself, its knob and its mounting plate. The wall it hangs on, switches and other
trades' work near it are room context: never compared.
