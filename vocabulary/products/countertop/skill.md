# Countertops

How GV reviews a countertop shop drawing. Read with `SKILL.md` (how to work) and `rules.md` (GV's
numbers in force for this set: they win over anything written here).

## What a countertop drawing shows
A plan (the top seen from above: wall to wall, pieces, seams, cut-outs, sink centre lines), elevations
(heights, splashes), and sections (thickness, overhang, edge profile). Which view to read: the
**plan** for cut-out positions, offsets and wall-to-wall; the **elevation** for heights and splashes;
the **section** for overhang and thickness.

## Compare
- **Sizes**: overall length, each piece, **depth** (countertop depth = cabinet depth + overhang),
  top and splash thickness, counter height above the floor.
- **Sink**: centre line from the wall or end, model or tag (SNK-...), undermount or drop-in, cut-out
  width and depth, faucet holes. Rim collars must fit their holes.
- **Backsplash and side splash**: present or not, height, thickness, material (stone or tile). A side
  splash only where the top meets a wall; a free-standing end gets none.
- **Overhangs and supports**: front and side overhang, knee space, brackets, corbels.
- **Cut-outs**: sink, cooktop, grommets, outlets.
- **Edges and seams**: edge profile, seam positions, field cut (extra length cut on site, per wall end).
- **Material tags**: CT-, ST-, SS-, QZ-, PL-, LA- and the like; the vendor's tag on a piece must match
  the architect's for the same piece.

## GV's standards (not on the drawing)
GV's countertop checks use values a person enters or GV's company standard: sink clearance each side
of the cut-out, the sink's front offset and the minimum back offset, the field cut per wall end, the
overhang and splash thickness. Each is in `rules.md` with where it comes from. A value nobody entered
is missing: say so, never assume one.
