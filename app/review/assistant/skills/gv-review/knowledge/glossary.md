# Glossary

Plain words for what millwork drawings say. Background only.

## The drawings
- **Architect's drawing, ID set, arch set**: the design the work must match.
- **Shop drawing**: the vendor's (fabricator's) build drawing. What GV checks.
- **Brand prototype, brand standard**: a brand's typical design copied onto a sheet. A reference,
  never this job's design; compare with it only as its own group.
- **Plan / elevation / section / detail**: seen from above / from the front / cut through / a
  close-up of one joint or edge.
- **Rev 1, rev 2 (round 1, round 2)**: the vendor's first and later submittal of the same item;
  the newest round is what the vendor proposes now.
- **Cloud**: a scalloped outline round a change or a comment. GV's clouds are GV's notes.
- **SIM**: similar (built like another one). **TYP**: typical (the same everywhere it applies).
- **NTS**: not to scale (never measure it). **DUP**: duplicate.

## Dimensions
- **Overall, O/A**: the whole length. **Chain, string**: the pieces in a row; they add up to the
  overall. **Locating dimension**: a distance to a thing (an outlet, a sink centre line); it is that
  thing's position, not a size.
- **AFF**: above finished floor. **CL, ℄**: centre line. **O.C.**: on centre (centre to centre).
- **CLR**: clear (the open space between faces). **EQ**: equal (the pieces share the length).
- **U/S**: underside. **T.O.**: top of. **B.O.**: bottom of.
- **VIF**: verify in field (to be measured on site). **U.N.O.**: unless noted otherwise.
- **NIC**: not in contract. **By others**: someone else supplies it.
- **mm [in]**: `660 [26]` is 660 millimetres, 26 inches. Inches count; the mm are a check.

## Cabinets
- **Carcass, box**: a cabinet's body. **Base / upper (wall) / tall**: floor, wall-hung, full height.
- **Toe kick, TK**: the recess at the floor under a base cabinet.
- **Filler**: a strip between a cabinet and a wall or another cabinet; cut on site to fit.
- **Scribe**: trimming a piece to an uneven wall. **Reveal**: a small planned gap or step.
- **Finished end, FIN END, end panel**: a visible cabinet side made to look finished.
- **Equipment cabinet**: one that holds a sink or an appliance; its size never changes.
- **Fronts**: doors, drawers, false fronts. **Pull-out**: a drawer or bin that slides out.
- **ADJ shelf**: adjustable shelf. **Cam lock**: a small key lock. **Grommet, GROM**: a cable hole.
- **DW / MW / REF / UC**: dishwasher / microwave / refrigerator / under-counter.

## Countertops
- **Top, deck**: the countertop. **Overhang**: how far the top passes the cabinet face.
- **Backsplash, BS**: the upstand at the back, sitting on the top. **Side splash**: the upstand at an
  end, only where the top meets a wall. A free-standing end gets none.
- **BS vs SS**: BS is backsplash, but SS is solid surface on some sheets and stainless steel on
  others: read the legend.
- **Field cut**: extra length added at the factory and trimmed on site (see field-cut-and-ends.md).
- **Pocket**: a slot in a wall that the stone end sits in.
- **Undermount / drop-in**: a sink fixed under the top / resting on it. **Rim, collar**: the edge
  ring of a sink or a fitting; it must be larger than its hole.
- **Cut-out**: the hole cut in the top (sink, cooktop, grommet).
- **Seam**: where two pieces of top meet.

## Materials and finishes
- **PLAM, HPL**: plastic laminate. **SSM**: solid surface. **QTZ**: quartz. **ST**: stone.
- **Veneer, WD**: wood. **MT**: metal. **Edge band**: the strip on a panel's edge.
- Tags like `CT-1`, `ST-02`, `PL-3`, `WD-01` name a finish in the schedule or legend.

## Electrical
- **Duplex**: a two-socket outlet. **GFCI**: a ground-fault outlet (near water). **J-box**: junction
  box. Outlet heights are usually given AFF to the outlet's centre line.
