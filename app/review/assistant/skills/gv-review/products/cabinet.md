# Cabinets

How GV reviews a cabinet (casework) shop drawing. Read with `SKILL.md` (how to work) and `rules.md`
(GV's numbers in force for this set: they win over anything written here).

## What a cabinet drawing shows
Elevations of each run (cabinet and filler widths left to right, heights, doors and drawers, tags), a
plan (the run's wall-to-wall and depths) and sections (carcass depth, door thickness, toe kick). Which
view to read: the **elevation** for widths, fillers and tags; the **plan** for wall to wall.

## Compare
- **Widths**: each cabinet and filler, left to right, and the run's overall (wall to wall).
- **Heights and depths**: base and tall heights, uppers' bottom and top, toe kick, depth (carcass
  depth + door thickness, about 3/4" for a door).
- **Fronts**: door or drawer, how many and how arranged, hinge side, open shelves, pull-outs, bins.
- **Panels and fillers**: end panels, scribes, fillers at walls and appliances (filler limits in
  `rules.md`); uppers present and where they start and stop.
- **Equipment cabinets** (sink, microwave, under-counter fridge, other appliances): their size must
  not change, and they sit where the architect puts them.
- **Hardware**: pulls (type, size, finish), hinges, locks, shelves fixed or adjustable.
- **Material tags**: finish and material of fronts, carcass and panels match the architect's.

## GV's standards (not on the drawing)
GV fits a run to the site by **adjusting fillers first**; equipment cabinets are never resized. The
filler limits and the cabinet width limits per kind (single door, double door, drawer) are in
`rules.md` with where each comes from. A value nobody entered is missing: say so, never assume one.
